What's new at CriticalIQ

Platform updates, definition changes, and news from the UK protection market.

February 2026 Launch

CriticalIQ is live

We're excited to launch the UK's most comprehensive protection comparison platform. Starting today, financial advisers can compare critical illness, life cover, and income protection definitions from 27+ UK providers — with access to 640+ historical policies going back to 1985.

February 2026 Feature

Income protection comparison now included

Income protection definitions are now fully integrated alongside critical illness comparisons. Compare deferred periods, benefit terms, and definition wordings across every major UK provider.

February 2026 Database

182 conditions now tracked

Our condition database has expanded to 182 critical illness conditions — the most comprehensive tracking in the UK market. From cardiac conditions to neurological, respiratory, musculoskeletal, and children's CI, every definition is monitored daily.

February 2026 Archive

Historical archive reaches 640+ policies

Our historical policy archive now includes 640+ policies from 1985 to present, covering legacy providers like Norwich Union, CGU, General Accident, Friends Provident, Bright Grey, Scottish Provident, and many more. Compare a client's existing cover against today's best.
