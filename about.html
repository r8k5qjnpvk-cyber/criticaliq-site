Why we built this

Critical illness definitions change constantly. A heart attack definition that paid out under a 2015 policy might not match today's wording — and vice versa. Providers add conditions, remove them, change severity thresholds, and update claim criteria without fanfare.

Advisers need to know exactly what's changed, when it changed, and how it affects their clients. That's what CriticalIQ does — we track every definition, from every provider, every single day.

Our historical archive goes back to 1985, covering legacy providers like Norwich Union, CGU, General Accident, Friends Provident, and many more. If your client took out a policy in the 1990s, we can show you exactly what it covered — and how far today's policies have come.
