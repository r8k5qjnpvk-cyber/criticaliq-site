Historical Archive

The 40-year story of CI cover

From 4 conditions in 1983 to 182 today. Understanding the evolution is essential for benchmarking old policies against new ones.

1983
Dread Disease Cover Invented
Dr Marius Barnard (South Africa) creates the first product covering 4 conditions: Heart Attack, Stroke, Cancer, Coronary Artery Bypass. Called "Dread Disease" for the conditions people most feared.
Origin · 4 conditions
4
UK
1985
Arrives in the UK
First CI policies issued to UK consumers. Original 4 conditions only. Sold exclusively through IFAs. Renamed "Critical Illness Cover" for more palatable marketing.
UK Launch
Late 80s–90s
The Conditions Race Begins
Insurers compete by adding conditions — MS, kidney failure, organ transplant, TPD. No standardisation. Definitions vary wildly. Advisers struggle to compare products meaningfully.
Expansion · 40+ conditions
40+
ABI
1999
ABI Statement of Best Practice v1
First industry-wide standards. Cancer, Heart Attack and Stroke mandated as minimum. Consistent definitions allow meaningful comparison for the first time.
ABI Standard v1
2003
Peak Sales Year — 992,000 policies
Highest-ever CI sales. Declining after due to rising premiums and mis-selling concerns. Standalone CI falls from 112k (2001) to 19k (2015). Adviser reputation for CI advice improves through the 2010s.
Market peak
992k
23
2006–07
ABI Publishes 23 Mandatory Model Definitions
Mandatory for ABI members from April 2007. Conditions split into two groups. World's clearest benchmark for adviser comparison. CIExpert launches in 2012 using this framework.
ABI Standard v2
2011
TPD Standardised for First Time
World's first standard Total Permanent Disability definitions. Previously 55% of TPD claims were declined. Standardisation dramatically improves outcomes. Major milestone.
ABI Standard v3
TPD
SIC
2014–15
Vitality Launches Severity-Based Cover
Serious Illness Cover — UK's first severity-based product. Partial payouts tied to medical severity not just diagnosis. Friends Life (now Aviva) adds mortgage payment option same year.
Innovation
2018–19
ABI Major Revision — New TPD Definitions
Most comprehensive update since 1999. New TPD definitions, updated cancer and partial payment rules. Mandatory from February 2019. Guardian 1821 re-enters market with unique "no severity for heart attacks" approach.
ABI Standard v4
v4

Heart Attack Redefined · 182 Conditions
ABI 2022 Standards (effective Sep 2023): heart attack definition tightened. Aviva acquires AIG Life (Apr 2024). Vitality SIC+ reaches 182 conditions. CriticalIQ tracks every change daily across 20+ providers.
