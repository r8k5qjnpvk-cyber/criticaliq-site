3. How We Use Your Data

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We share data only with: Square (payment processing), Resend (transactional email), Netlify (hosting). All processors are contractually bound to comply with UK GDPR.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, object to, or restrict processing of your data. Email hello@criticaliq.co.uk — we will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in. We may use analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics) to understand platform usage — these can be declined. We do not use advertising cookies.
