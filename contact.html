Common questions

How do I subscribe?

Click "Subscribe / Enter Code" on any page, then choose monthly (£19/mo) or annual (£189/yr, saving £39). Payment is handled securely via Square.

Can I get a group licence for my network?

Yes — we offer discounted group licences for adviser networks and larger firms. Email us with your network name and approximate number of advisers.

I have a suggestion or feature request

We actively build based on adviser feedback. Drop us an email with your idea — we read every message and prioritise features that help the most advisers.

Is CriticalIQ regulated?

CriticalIQ is a comparison and research tool — we don't provide financial advice. We are not regulated by the FCA. The tool is designed for use by authorised financial advisers to support their own research and client recommendations.
