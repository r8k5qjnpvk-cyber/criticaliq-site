Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 February 2026

We only use cookies that are necessary to run the site, and analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. We ask for your consent before setting analytics cookies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

CookieTypePurposeDuration
ciq_consentEssentialRemembers your cookie consent choice1 year

Essential cookies

The ciq_consent cookie is essential — it remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies so we don't ask you every time. This cookie does not track you.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics (G-PB2P882Y1M) to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular, how long people stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve the platform.

Google Analytics cookies are only set if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no data is collected.

Managing cookies

You can change your cookie preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site — the consent banner will reappear. You can also disable cookies in your browser settings.
